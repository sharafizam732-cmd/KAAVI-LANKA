Who we are

Kaavi Lanka is a Sri Lankan brand of handmade bags in natural linen. We make everyday pieces with the care of a craft studio and the eye of a fashion label.

What began as a love of art and textiles has grown into a collection of totes, handbags and shoulder bags.

Our philosophy

Three ideas guide every design.

Art

Every bag starts as a sketch, a colour or a texture we cannot stop thinking about.

Culture

We draw quietly on Sri Lankan craft and colour, never as decoration, always with respect.

Craft

Small batches, careful finishing and stitches that are meant to last.

Made from 100% natural linen

Linen is one of the oldest fabrics in the world, and one of the most honest. It is strong, breathable and gets softer the longer you own it.

We choose natural linen for its texture and for how well it ages, so your bag looks better with every year.

Our values

Quality

Fabric, thread and finishing are checked at every step.

Creativity

New shapes and colours, always with a reason.

Sustainability

Natural fibres, small batches and less waste.

Craftsmanship

Skilled hands behind every seam.
