The inspiration

Kaavi Lanka began with a simple wish: to make something beautiful that a woman could carry with her every day.

The colours of a morning market, the pattern of a woven mat, the quiet of a studio at dusk. Each of these became a starting point.

Art & culture

We look to Sri Lankan art and craft the way a painter looks at a landscape, for colour, rhythm and feeling.

The influence is subtle: a palette, a stitch, a proportion. Nothing is borrowed for show.
